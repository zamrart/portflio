Characters

Al Shamel

From a single concept to a complete rigged character.

THE PROJECT

A rabbit mascot in medical scrubs, developed from a client-supplied AI concept into a complete rigged 3D character.

MY CONTRIBUTION / FOCUS
Complete 3D character production

I built the character from scratch: modeling, topology, clothing, UVs, textures, fur, and rigging. The client supplied the visual concept; I translated it into a functioning 3D character.

The brief

The client supplied an AI-generated concept and asked for a fully functioning rigged character that matched the reference.

PROCESS & DESIGN

Making the reference work in three dimensions.

The work moves from the rabbit's proportions and facial forms into clothing construction, topology, surface development, and rig testing. The development images show how a single reference becomes a character with depth, separate components, and a poseable body.

Shape before surface

Head and body studies resolve the silhouette from several angles before fur and clothing add detail.

Clothing that belongs to the character

The garment versions move from a gathered waist to a separate top and drawstring trousers, with pockets, seams, and more relaxed folds.

A character that can perform

Face components, topology views, and rig tests connect the finished appearance to the requirements of posing and expression.
