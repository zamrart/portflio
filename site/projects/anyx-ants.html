Characters

ANYX

Wood, leaves, and a character taking shape.

THE PROJECT

A wooden ant character assembled from carved timber forms, wrapped rope, leaves, and woven fabric. The project follows the form from sculpted components to material studies and a reconstructed presentation.

MY CONTRIBUTION / FOCUS
3D character modeling

Translated supplied character concepts into optimized 3D models intended for rigging and animation, including a base model and character variations.

PROCESS & DESIGN

Turning a supplied design into assembled forms.

The original archive records the sculpt, separate limb and prop components, body assembly, and textured surfaces. New AI-assisted presentation images explore a complete assembled stance and a closer material view; they are interpretations of the surviving references, not recovered geometry.

Organic construction

Carved timber, wrapped rope, and leaf shapes make the ant feel assembled from natural materials.

Detail at two scales

Whole-body images establish the proportions; close material views show grain, weave, and the joins between parts.
