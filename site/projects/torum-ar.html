Torum / AR Characters

Character art that moves into real time.

THE PROJECT

A large AR character-production project: multiple character collections, expressions, accessories, real-time rendering, and application presentation.

MY CONTRIBUTION / FOCUS
Lead 3D Artist & Technical Artist

Modeling, texturing, blendshapes, and Unity implementation, including shaders, lighting, and post-processing. The role also included workflow research, outsourced-art feedback, and collaboration on optimization and tooling.

Unity

PROCESS & DESIGN

From character asset to an interactive face filter.

The work connects many character families to a shared AR pipeline. Models, surface treatment, blendshapes, and accessories had to retain each collection’s visual identity while working in real time. The motion gallery shows the finished range; original development and application captures show the production context.

A family of styles

The collection spans rounded cartoon faces, voxel characters, birds, and penguins, each with distinct silhouettes and accessories.

Expression in real time

Blendshapes, shaders, lighting, and Unity integration connect the character asset to an interactive face filter.

Production across a collection

My lead role included workflow research, outsourced-artist feedback, quality review, and engineering collaboration on performance.
