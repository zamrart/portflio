Medieval Blacksmith

A workshop with a Viking spirit.

THE PROJECT

A medieval blacksmith workshop built around weathered surfaces, hand-crafted tools, and the glow of the forge.

MY CONTRIBUTION / FOCUS
Modeling, texturing & rendering

Modeled in Maya and ZBrush, textured in Substance Painter, and rendered in V-Ray.

Maya · ZBrush · Substance Painter · V-Ray

PROCESS & DESIGN

Making a workshop feel used.

The scene brings together individually crafted tools and architectural details. The original project includes additional asset breakdowns and a demoreel.

Focal point

The bright forge and central work area pull attention through the darker interior.

Material storytelling

Weathered wood, metal tools, stone, and scattered props give the workshop a working history.
